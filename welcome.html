Welcome to the City

Welcome! If you have ended up here, theres a good chance you heard the call without even realizing it! This is the hub for all things Citywits, past present & future. Currently we are under construction & progress is slow. When completed, this site will act as an Archive to preserve the work that I have poured my heart & soul into over the years. The Citywits Legacy, if you will. Staff is sporadic throughout the organization, but, rest assured, the machines and I are all we need.

In addition to all Citywits work and back catalogue, there will also be vaults for my dormant two-man band (SMALL DRAWINGS) my solo & theatrical work, as well as songs produced for friends of the City at Citywits Studios. Additionally there will be vaults for all artwork and video content, everything. If it takes 15 years, this will be my end-of-the-world bunker.
