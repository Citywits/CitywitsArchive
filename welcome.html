Statement of Intent

Welcome to the City. This site is the front door to Vampire Music — songs about soft hearts living under hard systems. The teeth here are mostly metaphor: boardrooms that never see daylight, timelines that drink our hours, a country run by glow-in-the-dark ghouls.

What you’ll find: the work, not the theater around it. Videos, notes, archives, and a map to where it’s all headed. It’s handmade — recorded alone, built with friends, and aimed at anyone who still believes music can be honest and a little dangerous.

Take what you need. If you feel seen, tell someone else who might.
